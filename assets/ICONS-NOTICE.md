# Technology icons

The files in `assets/icons/` are unmodified copies of icons from **Simple Icons** (package `simple-icons`, version **16.35.0**,
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), vendored so the profile does not depend on a CDN. The Simple Icons data is
released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public-domain dedication); the licence text is at
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.35.0/LICENSE.md.

Icon files: `python`, `rust`, `typescript`, `nodedotjs`, `webgpu`, `vulkan`, `linux`, `githubactions`.

**Trademarks.** Each icon depicts a trademark or brand mark that belongs to its owner. Simple Icons asks all users to read its
legal disclaimer (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md). Here the marks are used only
nominatively, to name a technology that is actually used in the work shown, drawn in a single muted colour by
`scripts/generate-profile-assets.mjs`. Their use implies no affiliation with, sponsorship by, or endorsement from the owners.

Our own category glyphs (`assets/tiles/`) and the topology hero are original and not derived from any brand mark.
